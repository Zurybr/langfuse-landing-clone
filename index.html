Questions & Answers

What is Langfuse?

Langfuse is an open source platform for tracing, evaluating and prompting LLM applications. It captures hierarchical traces of every model call, tool call and retrieval, then lets teams score them with LLM-as-a-judge or human reviewers.

What does Langfuse help me with?

Debugging failures, comparing prompt or model changes, controlling cost and latency, and building golden datasets from real production traffic.

How does Langfuse compare to LangSmith, Braintrust, Arize, Galileo, and Datadog?

Langfuse is MIT licensed and self-hostable, uses OpenTelemetry as its foundation, and combines observability, prompt management and evals in one product instead of separate subscriptions.

Can I use just tracing without the other features?

Yes. Tracing works standalone; evaluation, prompts and experiments layer on top when you need them.

What deployment options do exist?

Langfuse Cloud, Docker Compose, Kubernetes via Helm, and Terraform modules for AWS, GCP and Azure.

Is self-hosting actually free?

Yes — self-hosted usage only pays for the infrastructure you run it on. The full feature set is available without a license fee.

What frameworks are supported?

Anything that can emit OpenTelemetry spans, plus 100+ ready-made integrations for LangChain, Vercel AI SDK, LiteLLM, Pydantic AI, CrewAI and more.

What's the latency impact?

Negligible. Ingestion is asynchronous with batching, and evaluation runs in the background — typically adding only a few milliseconds per request.

Is Langfuse secure and compliant?

SOC 2 Type II, GDPR compliant, SSO/SAML, RBAC, audit logs, encryption in transit and at rest, and self-hosting for full data residency control.

How do I get started?

Install an SDK or OTel exporter, point it at your project key, and send your first trace. Most teams are running in under five minutes.

How does pricing work?

Usage-based pricing per observation, with a generous free tier, volume discounts and annual enterprise agreements.
